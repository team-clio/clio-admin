## Radius & spacing

- Control radius: `8px` (`rounded-lg`)
- Surface radius: `12px` (`rounded-xl`)
- Modal radius: `16px` (`rounded-2xl`)
- Base spacing unit: `4px`
- Standard control height: 약 `36–40px`
